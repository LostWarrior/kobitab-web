What really decides it

A library or a shared drive?

Dropbox is stronger for file logistics.

If the goal is syncing files, replacing manual transfers, sharing folders, or backing up devices, Dropbox is the more complete product.

Kobi is stronger for personal context.

Kobi is designed around finding a remembered idea across saved links, local folders, and notes, then helping you do something useful with the result.

They can sit beside each other.

Dropbox can remain your cloud file layer. Kobi can stay focused on private retrieval and agent workflows over the material you choose to bring into your Mac library.

The honest answer

Choose by the center of gravity.

Choose Kobi if…

Your main problem is remembering and reusing private context across bookmarks, notes, and local material on your Mac.

Choose Dropbox if…

Your main problem is file sync, cloud storage, sharing, backup, or access from every device and browser.
