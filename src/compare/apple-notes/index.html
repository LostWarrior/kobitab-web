Kobi vs
Apple Notes

Apple Notes is the obvious everyday place to capture thoughts inside the Apple ecosystem. Kobi is different: it is a Mac memory layer for finding context across bookmarks, files, and notes, then turning that context into reviewed work.

Kobitab

A private recall layer for your Mac.

Search bookmarks, Downloads, local folders, and Obsidian from one place, with optional AI and focused agents.

  • Local-first index and core library
  • Search across saved web pages and local sources
  • Focused agents for briefs, plans, and reviewed actions
  • Built for recall, not note authoring
A Apple Notes

The native note app already on your devices.

Create, edit, organize, search, lock, and share notes across Apple devices with iCloud enabled.

  • Fast capture on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and iCloud.com
  • Search typed text, handwriting, scans, and image objects
  • Tags, folders, Smart Folders, attachments, and collaboration
  • Locked notes have stronger encryption boundaries

What really decides it

Where does the thought go next?

Apple Notes wins at capture.

If the job is writing a note on your phone, scanning a document, adding a checklist, or sharing a folder with someone, Apple Notes is the better product.

Kobi wins when the memory is scattered.

Kobi is useful when the answer lives across bookmarks, Downloads, local folders, and vault notes, and you need to find the thread without remembering where you put it.

These tools can work together.

Apple Notes can stay your quick capture surface. Kobi can sit beside it as the retrieval and agent layer for the broader private library on your Mac.

The honest answer

Choose by the job.

Choose Kobi if…

Your problem is recall across many saved sources, and you want focused agents that prepare reviewed work from your private Mac library.

Choose Apple Notes if…

You want the most convenient Apple-native place to capture, edit, scan, tag, lock, and share notes across devices.
