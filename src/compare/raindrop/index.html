Raindrop.io

Kobi is for people who want bookmarks and local knowledge searchable on their Mac. Raindrop.io is for people who want a polished bookmark collection synced across devices. The better choice starts with where you want your library to live.

What really decides it

Local depth or cloud reach?

Kobi searches beyond bookmarks.

Your bookmark about a subject can sit beside an Obsidian note and a PDF in Downloads. Kobi is designed to recall across those source boundaries without asking you to upload everything into a new cloud collection.

Raindrop follows you everywhere.

Raindrop.io has the stronger multi-device story: browser capture, mobile apps, synced collections, highlighting, and collaboration. If your library must be equally available on every device, that reach is decisive.

The privacy models are different, not binary.

Kobi keeps its core library local and makes AI optional. Raindrop stores private account data in the cloud and says it uses a subscription model with no ads, tracking, or sale of user data. Choose the model that matches your needs.

The honest answer

Choose by the life of your library.

Choose Kobi if…

Your useful context already lives on a Mac across bookmarks, folders, Downloads, and Obsidian—and local ownership matters more than access from every device.

Choose Raindrop.io if…

You want best-in-class bookmark capture, cross-device sync, highlights, collaboration, web archiving, or mobile access. Those are real strengths, not footnotes.
