What really decides it

Do you need to write or retrieve?

Obsidian is stronger for building the knowledge base.

If your main work is writing Markdown, linking ideas, maintaining a graph, and shaping a long-lived vault, Obsidian is the better primary tool.

Kobi is stronger when the source is not only notes.

Kobi is for the moment when the thing you remember could be a bookmark, local file, download, or Obsidian note, and you want a direct route to the source.

The best setup may use both.

Obsidian can stay the writing home. Kobi can index the vault alongside the rest of your private library and help agents prepare reviewable work from that wider context.

The honest answer

They are complements more than rivals.

Choose Kobi if…

Your main problem is retrieving and acting on private context across bookmarks, files, Downloads, and notes.

Choose Obsidian if…

Your main problem is writing, linking, organizing, and customizing a durable Markdown knowledge base.

Obsidian facts: plain-text notes, about Obsidian, local Markdown import, search, and Sync security.
